How it works

  1. Pick the occasion. Each one comes with its own song: Happy Birthday, For He's a Jolly Good Fellow, a spooky Mountain King for Halloween, or We Wish You a Merry Christmas.
  2. Type their name. The ball bounces from syllable to syllable in time with the piano and lands on their name, which lights up on the screen.
  3. Send it. Copy the link into any chat, or download the video to post it as a story. They tap once and the card sings.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no watermark on the card, no limit on how many you send. Ads on the site pay for it.

Does the person need an app?

No. The link opens in any phone or computer browser. They tap once to open the card, because browsers only play sound after a tap.

Where is the name stored?

Nowhere. The name and message are part of the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers never send to a server. Anyone with the link can open the card, so share it only with the person it's for.

Can I post it on Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp Status?

Yes. Use "Download as a video" to save a vertical 1080×1920 MP4 with the music in it, then post it like any other video.

Can I use these songs freely?

Yes. Happy Birthday to You, For He's a Jolly Good Fellow, Grieg's In the Hall of the Mountain King and We Wish You a Merry Christmas are in the public domain, and the piano is a public-domain recording, so the videos are yours to share.
